# Speaker selection and independent semantic references

The selected event population, identity policy and semantic quality thresholds remain open decisions. This procedure establishes a review interface; it does not supply verified speaker identities, independent annotations, or training-ready labels. Existing lexical candidates and the known false matches and omissions in the [reconciliation](readiness-inventory-reconciliation.md) remain unreviewed.

## Turn selection

For an individual immutable JSON source record with a nonempty `turns` list, review **every** turn in source order against the source and available identity evidence. Record a reviewer and, for each unique nonempty `turn_id`, its exact text SHA-256, `selection` (`include` or `exclude`), and a nonempty reason. Use `include` only for confidently identified target-speaker text under the chosen policy; do not guess ambiguous attribution. Resolve ambiguity and missing text before selection. Preserve the review and evidence separately, including the policy version and review date. Decisions can be revised only as a new review bound to the same or a new source version.

The review JSON has `source_record_sha256`, `reviewer`, and a `turns` array. Compute the source hash over `json.dumps(record, sort_keys=True, ensure_ascii=True, separators=(',', ':')).encode('utf-8')`; each `text_sha256` hashes the turn's UTF-8 text. Run `python -m speechprefix.transcript_processing.speaker_selection record.json review.json selected.json` to create a new file. The tool checks the full record hash, turn order, IDs, text hashes, complete decisions and reasons, and refuses unresolved or entirely excluded records. It joins included text with newline separators and records half-open code-point spans back to original turns. The review itself must be retained: the selected file does not contain excluded turns or their reasons. A selection labeled reviewed records the supplied decision, not independent proof of speaker identity or completeness. This tool does not process a corpus or automatically connect its output to existing flattened-text runners; those runners continue to mark attribution unverified.

## Independent reference procedure

Before inspecting lexical candidates, independent annotators should receive the same source version, selected text, turn evidence and a versioned topic/idea definition. Each should independently enumerate supported topics and more specific ideas, cite exact selected-text spans and turn IDs, and explicitly report uncertain or missing source material. Record annotator identity, date, instructions/version, source and selected-text hashes, evidence, uncertainty and whether an empty inventory was explicitly reviewed. Annotators must not see candidate outputs before completing their inventories. Preserve their separate inventories, then adjudicate disagreements with reasons; keep unresolved items and record adjudicator identity and version. Do not infer absence from an empty candidate list or failed extraction.

Sample across source formats, lengths, multiple speakers, no-match cases and known errors; retain the sampling frame and denominator. Compare candidate support and omissions to adjudicated references, reporting per-event counts and disagreements as well as aggregate precision and recall under a predeclared matching policy. Establish acceptance thresholds before assessing release readiness. References generated by the same extractor or derived from its candidates are not independent; matching exact quotations verifies provenance, not semantic correctness. Until real independent reviews and agreed thresholds exist, no semantic candidate is a validated training label.
