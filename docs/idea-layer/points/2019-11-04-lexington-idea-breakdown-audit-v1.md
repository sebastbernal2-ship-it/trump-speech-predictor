## Audit rule

An **idea** here is a distinct proposition, appeal, promise, or assessment Trump expressed—not just a broad subject word. A **story/event** is a real-world referent that may support an idea, but is not interchangeable with it. One topic can contain multiple ideas; one proposition can refer to multiple stories. Relatedness is not a merge rule. In particular, the impeachment proceeding, the Ukraine call/complaint, and allegations about Biden family dealings are tracked separately unless a later evidence-based entity-resolution step explicitly links them. Search queries must preserve those distinctions.

## Extractor revision diagnostic (not a quality pass)

The v1.0 54-label inventory was not used as truth. I revised the extractor prompt to specify concrete conversation points, explicitly separate independently emphasized claims even when they share an event, remove the 60-idea soft cap, and check for both under- and over-splitting. A first v2.5 prompt run returned 98 ideas: it became more specific but promoted asides/details (for example, interest earnings and an announced dog visit) into ideas. A v2.6 refinement added a “key point” salience gate and returned 58. It now separates `impeachment_overturn` from `schiff_fabrication` (the Ukraine conversation), but still merges the Biden/Ukraine and Biden/China allegations into `biden_foreign_profit`, among other residual granularity errors. Thus **the extractor is not fixed**; the two runs are single-model diagnostics on one speech, not a valid precision/recall comparison or a reviewed result.
