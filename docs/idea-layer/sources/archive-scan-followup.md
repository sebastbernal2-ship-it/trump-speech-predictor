# All the News and CC-News: follow-up evidence

Assessed 2026-11-17. This is a partial investigation; the full-inventory Parquet scan and item-level clearance remain outstanding. No articles are cleared for use by this note.

The retained All the News mirror has 36 Parquet files and its acquisition reconciliation reports 2,688,878 rows. The CC-News mirror has five files and reports 708,241 rows. Those are acquisition reports, not fresh full scans or proof of corpus completeness. Their pinned identities and file digests are in the respective local `SOURCE-MANIFEST.json` files. The [prior coverage screen](full-inventory-coverage.md) explicitly excludes both archives. Its four inventory views overlap, so their rows must not be added as distinct speeches.

A scan could not be run in this environment: neither DuckDB nor PyArrow is installed in the project environment, and an attempt to install DuckDB failed because the package host could not be resolved. No coverage count should be inferred from that failure. Once a Parquet reader is available, scan every retained shard's date column, report invalid and missing dates and per-view and per-year hits for the seven prior calendar days, and include shard counts and input digests. Keep the same-day exclusion and distinguish this screening window from an event-specific cutoff. Inspect schema and date formats first; do not silently discard unparsable values. Compare results against the acquisition row totals and investigate discrepancies.

## Rights and timing evidence

* The retained [All the News mirror card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rjac/all-the-news-2-1-Component-one) describes the collection but does not establish permission from its publishers for the intended research, model training, or redistribution. The original dataset link now redirects to the Components homepage; it did not yield applicable terms in this investigation. Publisher-specific permission or governing terms remain necessary for candidate uses.
* The [CC-News mirror card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/vblagoje/cc_news) describes a subset of Common Crawl news, not a rights grant for each article. [Common Crawl's terms](https://commoncrawl.org/terms-of-use), last updated March 7, 2024, state that crawled material may be subject to its owners' separate terms and require respect for third-party rights. These terms do not establish the rights needed for any particular candidate. Record rights-holder, permitted uses, evidence, and date before changing the hold.
* [Common Crawl's news dataset description](https://commoncrawl.org/blog/news-dataset-available) identifies original WARC files; a dated capture of the relevant version could provide independent evidence that it existed by a cutoff. It is not enough to find a URL or the article's retrospective publication date: resolve a specific capture, retain a digest and provenance, compare its contents to the candidate version, and independently establish the event's start time and time zone. A later capture cannot prove the earlier version. No candidate-level capture, event cutoff, or rights authorization was established here.

The [previous audit](input-source-audit.md) reported 655,456 dated 2019 All the News rows and only two 2019-dated CC-News rows, plus 77,598 blank CC-News dates; these are historical audit results, not a substitute for the requested inventory scan. Both sources remain on hold for use as prediction inputs. No rights holder was contacted and no permission was obtained.
