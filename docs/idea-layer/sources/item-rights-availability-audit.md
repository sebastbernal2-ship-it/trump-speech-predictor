## Follow-up: independent evidence search

On 2026-11-17, a targeted public-source search covered the two pilot candidates above, not every item in the underlying collections. No candidate is cleared. The available evidence narrows one timing question but does not satisfy all gates:

* **Event:** [PBS](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/watch-live-trump-holds-rally-in-kentucky-on-the-eve-of-elections) reported on November 4 that the rally was *expected* at 7 p.m.; [LEX 18](https://www.lex18.com/news/covering-kentucky/trump-campaign-announces-rally-in-lexington) also reported a 7 p.m. scheduled rally. These are schedules, not observation of the actual beginning of Trump's remarks. The official [presidential digest](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/DCPD-2019DIGEST/html/DCPD-2019DIGEST.htm) places his remarks in the evening but does not give an exact start in the identified entry. Actual start and cutoff remain unverified; continue excluding the event date.
* **Post:** A [May 22, 2019 newspaper page](https://cdnek.ekirikas.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/0522_22_11969.pdf) displays a screenshot of a post attributed to the same account with the same text as the local candidate. [CBS/AP coverage](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-endorsed-kentucky-gov-matt-bevin-wins-gop-primary-today-2019-05-21/) also reproduces that text and displays an update time of May 21, 2019, 11:22 p.m. EDT. These are independent contemporary corroboration of the text, subject to the pages' own provenance and revision histories; neither inspected view establishes the specific post ID. The newspaper screenshot displays 8:25 a.m. on May 21 without a verified timezone. A [contemporary Reddit archive](https://www.reddit.com/r/TweetArchiver/comments/brbqod) reports 15:25:09 UTC, while the local archive states 23:25 without a timezone. Do not normalize or resolve this discrepancy by assumption. The search did not obtain a hashed, independently dated capture linking the exact post ID and version. Publication or reproduction does not establish rights to retain, train on, or redistribute the post. **Hold.**
* **Politico article:** The candidate URL was inaccessible to the search tool because of the site's robots policy. The search did not resolve a dated capture of the exact article version or its original source-row digest, nor did it obtain a use-specific grant. A separate contemporary article about the same election would not verify this candidate's version. **Hold.**

The linked pages are evidence leads viewed through a search service, not locally retained immutable captures; their displayed dates and text have not been independently authenticated. Rights holders, jurisdiction, applicable terms and permissions for the intended research, model-training and distribution uses remain undetermined for both candidates. No rights holder was contacted. Obtain a versioned capture and digest for each exact item, reconcile post identity and time, establish the observed speech start, and secure or assess use-specific rights before changing either disposition.
