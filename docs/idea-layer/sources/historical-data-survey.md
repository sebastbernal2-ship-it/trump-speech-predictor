# Historical news and social data survey

Surveyed 2026-11-17. This is an inventory, not a selection of prediction inputs. The existing seven-day headline benchmark remains frozen; subsequent input choices belong to a later phase. Local observations below rely on the earlier [source audit](input-source-audit.md), its retained manifests, and a read-only inspection of the external data directory. Counts are not a new corpus-wide validation. The audit's 38-event windows are a small historical snapshot, not coverage of the full speech corpus.

A stated date range is the range reported in the earlier audit, not a guarantee of complete coverage or of contemporaneous availability. Acquisition did not establish permission to redistribute underlying content.

## Timing and remaining gaps

Publication or post creation timestamps are **not proof** that a particular version was publicly available before a speech. Unless supported by independent contemporaneous evidence, publication time, time zone, first availability, revisions, deletion timing, archive completeness and historical engagement are **unverified**. A later crawl cannot establish earlier visibility; even an earlier capture proves only what that capture shows. Date-only records cannot safely establish ordering within the event day. Preserve immutable source records, original timestamps and retrieval/capture dates when later assessing any evidence.

The survey does not establish broad multi-outlet coverage for 1988, completeness for the 2022-01-29 event, or historical social coverage beyond the listed accounts. It does not measure public attention or virality: current engagement counts and article counts cannot establish either. Source rights, availability by event cutoff, costs of any full retrieval and actual coverage across the complete transcript inventory remain open. No paid access, download, collection or model-input decision was made here.
